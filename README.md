# 寶素名言秘錄（臺灣國家圖書館 06117） / Bao su ming yan mi lu [Collected Medical Sayings of Baosu]: Transcription Dataset

《寶素名言秘錄》是臺灣國家圖書館藏清代舊鈔本，館方著錄蔣寶素著。上下兩冊依病候分條，並有目次、短評、方藥與圖式。可追查條目編排、圖說與抄讀標記。

This Qing manuscript in the National Central Library, Taiwan, is attributed to Jiang Baosu. Its two bindings organize medical discussions by condition, with contents lists, observations, formula notes and small diagrams.

69頁都有定位。第6、7頁影像內容重複，讀序保留一次，兩頁各有來源定位。下冊眼圖另作標記。

All 69 source pages are indexed. Pages 6 and 7 reproduce the same scan content and are cross-referenced to one transcription. A small eye diagram in the lower binding is identified separately.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

69幅逐幅回圖，按原圖與初錄核對並附訂正紀錄。採用 6 個可回圖的讀法調整。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。按固定 PDF 與左右葉之欄定位，小字、藏印、書面缺損與疑讀保留分層。

All 69 images were reviewed. The revision documents 6 source-image-supported changes. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Fixed PDF positions and facing-leaf columns anchor the text. Small writing, seals, physical damage and uncertain readings are distinguished.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
